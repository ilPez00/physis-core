# Physis product boundary

## Open / research layer

Physis Research + Physis Core answer:

> Which mechanisms are justified by evidence?

This is the layer of matched nulls, permutation controls, negative results
and reproducibility artifacts. Its outputs are measurements and minimal
implementations, usable without an account, without a cloud, without
proprietary infrastructure.

Grant-safe work packages live here: benchmark datasets, retrieval and
structural-inference experiments, corpus-property measurement, deterministic
representations, provenance and replay mechanisms, research reference
implementations, reproducibility infrastructure.

RESEARCH work package: "Determine when explicit structure can replace or
constrain expensive learned inference."

## Commercial product layer

Physis answers:

> How do we make those mechanisms continuously useful on a real person's or
> organization's changing data?

Paid value lives in *continuity and operation*, not in the mechanism:
continuous ingestion; connectors and their maintenance; automatic corpus
profiling and pipeline selection; organization memory; the human/agent shared
workspace; hosted context service; managed deployment; enterprise search;
temporal organizational memory; multi-user provenance; workflow automation;
agent execution; dashboards; scale; monitoring; industrial adapters; support;
security; compliance; private deployment; SLAs.

PRODUCT work package: "Continuously apply those mechanisms to a customer's
changing systems and data."

## The one-line distinction

> Research discovers which mechanism works.
> Core implements the minimal reproducible mechanism.
> Product decides when, where and how to apply it continuously to real systems.
